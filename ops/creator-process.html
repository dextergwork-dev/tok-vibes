The process

  1. 1

    Source

    Ops
    1. Take the creator profile from the brief, like "mom with kids".
    2. Search Backstage, Instagram, X, LinkedIn or our Google Form.
    3. Add them to the Creator Database with Client, Portfolio, Email, Source, Scope and Rate.
    4. Write the Scope for this client. It goes straight into the outreach email.
    5. Best fits go to shortlist. This sends the outreach email automatically. Good extras go to backup.
    Creatornew creatorshortlistbackup
  2. 2

    Offer

    Ops

    New Production Engine client? Do this before you shortlist anyone

    • Open Make.com, folder 2026 UGC AUTOMATION, scenario Shortlist Auto-Email (ClickUp).
    • Change the subject line for this client.
    • Swap Example 1 and Example 2 for this client's brief examples. They are still the Ivy ones.
    • Check the client is in the brand list. Earthling Co and Hommey are missing, so they would show as a number.
    • Update the Scope on each creator in ClickUp. The email pulls it from there.
    • Shortlist yourself first as a test and check the email.

    The email that goes out when a creator hits shortlist:

    Subject

    Hey Creator Name,

    I hope you're doing well!

    We wanted to reach out because we think you'd be a great fit for a paid UGC opportunity we have available.

    Here's what we're working on:

    Brand: Client

    Scope: Scope from ClickUp

    To give you an idea of what to expect, here are two example briefs:

    • Example 1
    • Example 2

    Please note that these are only samples to show the structure, level of detail, and expected deliverables. Your actual brief will be customized specifically for this brand and won't be finalized until closer to filming.

    Most briefs include two talking-to-camera concepts plus 10 to 15 B-roll clips, though the exact requirements may vary depending on the campaign.

    If you're interested, simply reply YES to this email and we'll send over the next steps.

    Looking forward to hearing from you!

    Warm regards,
    The Tok-Vibes Team

  3. 3

    Contract and onboard

    Ops
    1. Send the creator contract in DocuSign.
    2. Once signed, link the creator to their batch tasks.
    3. Make their Drive footage folder and paste it in Raw footage link.
    💡Always keep a backup. If a creator goes quiet, swap in the next one the same day.
    Creatorcontract sentready to onboardactive
  4. 4

    Brief

    Strategist writes · CD approves
    1. Strategist writes the brief and pastes it in Brief Link.
    2. CD reviews. Notes go back to the strategist.
    3. Ops sends the approved brief and Drive folder to the creator.
    Batchbriefingbrief reviewbrief feedbacksend to creator
  5. 5

    Film

    Creator films · Ops checks
    1. Creator films to the brief and uploads to their Drive folder.
    2. Ops checks every script and the B-roll are there and usable.
    3. Stuck or silent? Chase once, then swap to a backup.
    Batchrecordingproduction stuck
  6. 6

    Edit

    Editor cuts · Strategist approves
    1. CD assigns the editor and tags them in a comment.
    2. Editor posts the Frame.io link. We review within 5 to 8 hours.
    3. Once approved, the editor drops finals in Drive, one folder per batch.
    Batchready to editeditingedit reviewedit feedbackapproved - prep files
  7. 7

    Deliver and pay

    Ops
    1. Files to the client Drive and tracker within 30 to 60 minutes of approval.
    2. Sent to the client: the batch is launched.
    3. Send the creator the invoice form. They submit it at least 1 day before the pay date.
    4. Finance pays on the next run, marks it Paid, and the creator gets a confirmation email.
    5. Archive the creator.
