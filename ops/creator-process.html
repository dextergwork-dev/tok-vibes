Creator Sourcing to Delivery

How we find a creator, get them filming, and ship finished ads to the client. Seven stages, one owner each, and the ClickUp status that proves where things stand.

Creators: Creator Database Batches: client sprint list Template: [15] Production Engine Contracts: DocuSign Footage: Google Drive Review: Frame.io
  1. 1

    Source

    Owner Ops
    1. Take the creator profile from the brief (e.g. "mom with kids", "with kids but no face").
    2. Search Backstage, Instagram, X, LinkedIn or our Google Form.
    3. Add each one to the Creator Database. Fill in Client, Portfolio, Email, Source, Scope and Rate.
    4. Best fits go to shortlist. Good extras go to backup.
    Creatornew creatorshortlistbackup
  2. 2

    Outreach and offer

    Owner Ops

    Reach out, then send the offer in plain terms. The Ivy offer as an example:

    • 2 short scripted videos (full brief provided) + 10 to 15 B-roll clips
    • Phone-filmed and natural. No studio, ring light or editing
    • Product purchases needed for the shoot are reimbursed
    • Perpetual usage rights, organic and paid, on brand channels. No whitelisting unless agreed
    • $200 for 2 scripts + B-roll

  3. 3

    Contract and onboard

    Owner Ops
    1. Send the creator contract in DocuSign.
    2. Once signed, link the creator to their batch tasks (Creator field).
    3. Create their raw footage folder in Drive and paste it in Raw footage link on each batch.

    Always keep a backup. If a locked creator goes quiet, swap in the next one the same day.

    Creatorcontract sentready to onboardactive
  4. 4

    Brief

    Owner Strategist · approves CD
    1. Strategist writes the brief and pastes it in Brief Link.
    2. CD reviews. Notes send it back to the strategist.
    3. Approved brief and Drive folder go to the creator (Ops sends).
    Batchbriefingbrief reviewbrief feedbacksend to creator
  5. 5

    Film

    Owner Creator · chases Ops
    1. Creator films to the brief and uploads raw footage to their Drive folder.
    2. Ops checks it is complete (every script plus B-roll) and usable.
    3. Stuck, silent or unusable footage: chase once, then swap to a backup.
    Batchrecordingproduction stuck
  6. 6

    Edit

    Owner Editor · approves Strategist
    1. CD assigns the editor and tags them in a comment.
    2. Editor cuts and pastes the Frame.io link. We review within 5 to 8 hours.
    3. Strategist approves or sends notes. Editor drops finals in Drive, one subfolder per batch (e.g. B0036).
    Batchready to editeditingedit reviewedit feedbackapproved - prep files
  7. 7

    Deliver and pay

    Owner Ops
    1. Final files into the client Drive and tracker within 30 to 60 minutes of approval.
    2. Files sent to the client: batch is launched.
    3. Pay the creator their rate plus approved reimbursements, then archive them.
