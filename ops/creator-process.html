The process

  1. 1

    Source

    Ops
    1. Take the creator profile from the brief, like "mom with kids".
    2. Search Backstage, Instagram, X, LinkedIn or our Google Form.
    3. Add them to the Creator Database with Client, Portfolio, Email, Source, Scope and Rate.
    4. Best fits go to shortlist. Good extras go to backup.
    Creatornew creatorshortlistbackup
  2. 2

    Offer

    Ops

    Reach out and send the offer in plain terms. Here's the Ivy offer:

    Deliverables2 scripted videos + 10 to 15 B-roll clips
    FilmingPhone, natural, no editing needed
    PurchasesReimbursed
    UsagePerpetual, organic and paid. No whitelisting
    Rate$200
    Creatorin conversationnot interested - rateno responsedisqualified
  3. 3

    Contract and onboard

    Ops
    1. Send the creator contract in DocuSign.
    2. Once signed, link the creator to their batch tasks.
    3. Make their Drive footage folder and paste it in Raw footage link.
    💡Always keep a backup. If a creator goes quiet, swap in the next one the same day.
    Creatorcontract sentready to onboardactive
  4. 4

    Brief

    Strategist writes · CD approves
    1. Strategist writes the brief and pastes it in Brief Link.
    2. CD reviews. Notes go back to the strategist.
    3. Ops sends the approved brief and Drive folder to the creator.
    Batchbriefingbrief reviewbrief feedbacksend to creator
  5. 5

    Film

    Creator films · Ops checks
    1. Creator films to the brief and uploads to their Drive folder.
    2. Ops checks every script and the B-roll are there and usable.
    3. Stuck or silent? Chase once, then swap to a backup.
    Batchrecordingproduction stuck
  6. 6

    Edit

    Editor cuts · Strategist approves
    1. CD assigns the editor and tags them in a comment.
    2. Editor posts the Frame.io link. We review within 5 to 8 hours.
    3. Once approved, the editor drops finals in Drive, one folder per batch.
    Batchready to editeditingedit reviewedit feedbackapproved - prep files
  7. 7

    Deliver and pay

    Ops
    1. Files to the client Drive and tracker within 30 to 60 minutes of approval.
    2. Sent to the client: the batch is launched.
    3. Pay the creator plus approved reimbursements, then archive.
    BatchlaunchedCreatorpaid - archived
