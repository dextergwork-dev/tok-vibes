# Client Update

Posted Monday, Wednesday and Friday in each `brand-client` channel.

## Rules

- Lead with what the client gets: what's ready and what it lets them do.
- Use their first name. Thank them for something specific.
- Only good news and next steps. If we need something, it's one friendly ask with a reason that helps them.
- Never explain what went wrong. Say what's coming and when.
- 5 to 8 lines. No links.

## Strategy Engine version

```
Morning [First name]! ☀️

Fresh batch for you: *[#] new briefs* are in your Notion and ready for your editors. [One line on the angle, e.g. "Built around the sleep-anxiety hooks that won last week."]

*Ready now*
• [#] briefs ready to edit
• [#] creatives your team produced this week, great pace 🙌

*Coming next*
• [#] more briefs by [day]

*A quick favor* (only if needed)
Could you [ask] by [day]? That keeps your next batch landing on time.

Love what we're building together. Anything you want more of, just say the word!
```

## Blocker phrasing (turn the problem into a next step)

| Situation | Say |
|---|---|
| Waiting on their feedback | "Could you share notes on the [#] edits by [day]? That gets them to you ready to launch by [day]." |
| Missing assets | "Could you drop [asset] in when you get a sec? It unlocks [#] more creatives." |
| Creator footage late | "Fresh creator footage lands [day], so [#] more creatives follow by [day]." |
| Editor capacity | "The next [#] creatives are on track for [day]." |
| Brief not approved | "Could you give the [#] new briefs a quick thumbs up by [day]? Editing starts right after." |
