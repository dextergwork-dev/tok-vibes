# New Strategist Onboarding Runbook

Say "Onboard [name] as a strategist" and Claude runs this. Claude drafts, you review and send.

Full overview: [New Strategist Onboarding](https://claude.ai/artifact/PpNvgvMsRB1VkE6ApwWFB2) · [ClickUp page](https://app.clickup.com/90121983842/docs/2kxv0nv2-772/2kxv0nv2-10292)

## What Claude needs from you

- Full name and personal email
- Start date
- First brand (from the Creative Director)
- Rate (default ~$1,500/mo for one brand, one-month trial)

## Steps

| # | Step | Owner | Claude does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Offer call: trial + growth path (1 → 2 → 3 brands) | Oli | Adds them to the tracker below |
| 2 | Create Tok-Vibes email | Ops | Reminds you, then waits for the address |
| 3 | Send DocuSign contract to personal email | Ops | Drafts the envelope from the contract template for you to send |
| 4 | Send welcome email (Day 1) | Ops | Drafts it in Gmail from `2-messages.md` |
| 5 | Invite to Slack + ClickUp, share Notion | Ops | Lists the invites to send, posts Slack welcome draft |
| 6 | Tutorials: ClickUp + Notion Looms | Strategist | Adds a ClickUp task with due date Day 3 |
| 7 | Intro call with Oli + CD | Oli / CD | Drafts the invite note |
| 8 | Assign first brand, grant Meta ad account + Dashboard + Frame.io | CD / Ops | Creates `#[brand]-strategy-[name]` channel draft and access checklist |
| 9 | First sprint, CD reviews every brief | CD | Adds a weekly check-in task |
| 10 | Trial review at 1 month | Oli / CD | Sets a reminder 3 days before |

## Access checklist

- [ ] Tok-Vibes email
- [ ] Slack
- [ ] ClickUp
- [ ] Notion
- [ ] Frame.io
- [ ] Ad Strategist Dashboard
- [ ] Meta ad account (first brand)
- [ ] Invoice form link sent (Finance: Mary)
