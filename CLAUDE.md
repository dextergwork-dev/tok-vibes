## How we write to people (always)

Every message to a client, editor, strategist or UGC creator is written Dale Carnegie style. This applies to Slack, email and any draft, for every client.

- Open warm and use their first name.
- Give honest, specific appreciation for something they did.
- Lead with what's in it for them: what's ready, what it unlocks, how it helps.
- Never criticize, blame or point out who got it wrong. Say what's next.
- Respect their view first ("You're right that...", "Fair question"), then explain.
- Make asks easy: one clear ask, a reason that helps them, a date. Clients get "no rush".
- Let them feel ownership ("Would that work for you?" instead of "You need to").
- End warm and encouraging.
- Client channel: open with "hey @channel!", always "we", never name anyone on our team.
- No em dashes. Short and plain.
- Drafts go in the chat for Dexter to send. Never post or save drafts in Slack or Gmail unless asked.
